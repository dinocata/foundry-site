Your data, explained

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy covers Foundry for iPhone and this website. Swift d.o.o. is responsible for the personal information described here. Contact us at hello@swift.hr.

Your training stays on your device. Foundry does not require an account or a network connection to record workouts or review your history. We do not receive your workouts or profile entries through the app.

1. Information stored on your iPhone

Workouts, sets, weights, repetitions, body measurements, profile answers, training goals, custom exercises, equipment, and preferences are stored locally. Foundry does not upload them to a workout server.

Exporting creates files that you can share or save to a location you choose. Those files can contain sensitive information, including profile details and measurements. The service or person you share them with handles that copy under their own terms. Device backups you choose to use are also subject to your Apple settings.

2. Crash reporting

Foundry uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and errors. Reports can contain crash traces, diagnostic messages, app and operating-system versions, device information, timestamps, and installation identifiers. Crash reporting is on by default and can be switched off in Preferences.

Foundry attaches a randomly generated profile identifier to crash reports. The identifier is created on first launch and included in your backup archive, so restoring a backup can associate reports from more than one device with the same profile. It is not your name or email address. Foundry shows it as Report ID in Preferences › Privacy.

3. Usage analytics

Google Analytics for Firebase helps us understand which screens and features are used and improve the app. When enabled, it receives screen visits and product interactions, such as creating a workout or saving an edit, together with technical information such as app version, device and operating-system information, app-instance identifiers, and an approximate region derived by Google from the network connection. Foundry does not request your precise location for analytics.

Enabled analytics uses the same random profile identifier described above. Events do not contain the names, notes, weights, repetitions, body measurements, sex, birth year, exercise selections, or other training values you enter. Foundry does not send demographic user properties.

“Share usage data” in Preferences controls analytics. It starts on in the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Singapore, Brazil, and Mexico, and off elsewhere. The country comes from the App Store storefront, then the device region; an unknown country is treated as outside that list. Your explicit choice takes priority, subject to the age restriction below. Turning sharing off disables analytics and removes the profile identifier from it; the identifier itself stays on your device as your Report ID. Turning sharing off does not delete reports already received.

Outside the listed countries, a recorded birth year that may mean you are under 18 keeps analytics off through the end of the year you turn 18. The birth year remains on your device.

4. Update checks

Foundry uses Firebase Remote Config to fetch minimum supported app versions on launch and when returning to the foreground, subject to caching. Google receives a Firebase installation identifier and technical connection information for this service. It operates independently of the analytics and crash-reporting switches and sends no workout or profile entries.

A failed fetch does not prevent offline access. Only a configuration fetched during the current launch can require an update.

5. Why we process this information

We use information to respond to support requests, diagnose faults, maintain compatibility, and improve Foundry. Where applicable, support processing is needed to answer your request, optional analytics relies on your consent, and essential update checks and permitted diagnostics serve our legitimate interests in maintaining a reliable app. You can withdraw an analytics choice at any time in Preferences.

Foundry has no advertising. We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled for Foundry’s analytics.

6. Service providers and international processing

Google provides Firebase Crashlytics, Analytics, and Remote Config. Data sent to those services can be processed outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area, under Google’s applicable data-processing terms and transfer safeguards. See Firebase privacy and security and Google’s privacy policy.

If you email us, Google’s Gmail service handles the message, your address, and anything you choose to include so we can respond. TestFlight beta distribution and feedback are handled by Apple under Apple’s TestFlight privacy information.

7. How long information is kept

Your training data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Exported archives and device backups remain wherever you choose to keep them.

Google Analytics event data is retained for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months. The user-level retention period resets when new activity is received, so it can extend while you continue using Foundry with analytics enabled. Google removes expired data through its scheduled deletion process. These settings do not apply to standard aggregate reports, which may remain available for longer.

Google states that Crashlytics keeps crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Firebase installation identifiers used by Remote Config are retained until a deletion request through the relevant Firebase API. Deletion from provider systems may take additional time under their published procedures.

We keep support correspondence while needed to resolve the request and handle related follow-up or legal obligations. You can contact us to request its deletion.

8. Your choices and requests

You can switch off analytics and crash reporting separately in Preferences, and export a copy of your local data. Removing the app deletes its on-device storage; independently saved backups are not removed. Disabling reporting or deleting the app does not automatically delete information already received by Google.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information we hold, or to object to or restrict processing where applicable, email hello@swift.hr. Include the Report ID shown in Preferences › Privacy. It is random and not linked to your email, so it is how we find your crash reports and analytics. We will explain what is needed and any applicable limits. Do not email a full training backup just to identify yourself.

Where applicable, you can withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing and complain to your local data-protection authority. We cannot access or erase your on-device workouts remotely.

9. This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies. GitHub receives technical request information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the site under the GitHub privacy statement.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this page when Foundry’s privacy practices change and revise the date above. Questions about this policy can be sent to Swift d.o.o. at hello@swift.hr.
