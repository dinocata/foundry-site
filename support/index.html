Support and privacy requests are handled by Swift d.o.o.

Report a problem

Tell us what you expected, what happened, and the steps that led to it. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and Foundry version from About if you can.

A screenshot can help. Please remove any personal information you do not want to share. You do not need to send a workout backup or body measurements to ask for help.

Backups and restoring

Foundry keeps your training data on your iPhone. Use Export Backup from the Workout tab’s settings menu before deleting the app or moving to another device. Keep the archive somewhere you control; the spreadsheet exports cannot be restored into Foundry.

We do not hold a server copy of your workouts and cannot recover them for you if the on-device copy and your backups are lost.

Privacy requests

Email the same address with “Foundry privacy request” in the subject. Describe your request; we will explain any information needed to identify the relevant records. Do not send your full backup unless we have discussed why it is needed.

See the privacy policy for details about on-device storage, Firebase services, and your controls.

Testing with TestFlight?

You can also send feedback from Foundry’s page in the TestFlight app. Apple provides the feedback and diagnostic information associated with that submission.
